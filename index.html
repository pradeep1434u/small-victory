The four levels

🟢 Win

Your minimum. A real success.

🔵 Big Win

You went beyond your minimum.

🟡 Great Win

You went substantially further.

🟣 Outstanding Win

You went far beyond your minimum. Enjoy it—don't turn it into a new minimum.
